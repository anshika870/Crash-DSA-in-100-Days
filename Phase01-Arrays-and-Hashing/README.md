# 🧩 Phase 01 — Arrays & Hashing

**Building the foundation of problem-solving, one element at a time.**

Welcome to Phase 01 of **Crash DSA in 100 Days**!

This phase focuses on mastering Arrays and Hashing — two fundamental concepts that appear frequently in coding interviews and form the foundation for many advanced problem-solving patterns.

The objective is to understand the underlying logic, recognize recurring patterns, and implement efficient solutions using Java.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By completing this phase, I aim to:

- Master array traversal, manipulation, and indexing.
- Understand HashMaps and HashSets and their applications.
- Learn frequency counting and complement-based searching.
- Understand prefix sums and subarray problems.
- Apply sorting and two-pointer techniques where relevant.
- Analyze and optimize time and space complexity.
